Why Southern Peripheral Road (SPR Road) is the Most Demanding Location in Gurugram

The Southern Peripheral Road has quickly emerged as one of the most desirable luxury real estate destinations in the NCR region. Several factors contribute to its rapid demand and investment potential.

  • Direct access to Golf Course Extension Road
  • Seamless link to Sohna Road
  • Close proximity to NH‑48
  • Easy connectivity to Indira Gandhi International Airport

Massive infrastructure investments have transformed SPR into a high-growth corridor. Wide roads, modern urban planning, and new commercial developments are attracting top developers and luxury brands.

SPR Road has become home to multiple ultra-premium developments, including Trump Towers Gurugram, making the corridor a preferred destination for luxury buyers.
