Over the past few years, Southern Peripheral Road has emerged as one of the most promising real estate corridors in the National Capital Region. The road connects key residential and commercial hubs, making it a top choice for luxury developments like Lamborghini Residences Gurugram.

Strategic Connectivity

SPR Road acts as a major link between Gurugram’s important business zones.

It offers seamless access to:

  • Golf Course Extension Road
  • Sohna Road
  • NH-48

This strategic connectivity significantly reduces travel time to key employment hubs.

Rapid Infrastructure Growth

The government and private developers have heavily invested in improving infrastructure around SPR Road. Wider roads, flyovers, and improved urban planning have accelerated the area’s transformation into a luxury residential destination.

Rising Demand for Premium Housing

The corridor is attracting luxury developments and branded residences, making it a preferred location for HNIs and NRI investors.

The increasing demand is driven by:

  • Modern urban planning
  • Proximity to corporate hubs
  • Limited supply of ultra-luxury homes

Close to Corporate and Business Districts

Residents living on SPR Road enjoy easy connectivity to major business hubs like:

  • Cyber City
  • Udyog Vihar
  • Golf Course Road

This makes SPR one of the most strategically located residential corridors in NCR.
